Maldives

# The Maldives – Discovering an Island Country This project presents the Maldives, a country located in the Indian Ocean, famous for its beautiful islands, white sandy beaches and crystal-clear waters. Throughout this project, we will learn about its geographical location, economy, people's daily lives, traditions, religion, official language, currency and some interesting facts. The aim is to learn more about this country, its culture, its main characteristics and the importance of tourism to its economy.
